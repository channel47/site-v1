# Creative research starter

A small, reusable version of the workflow in [Building a creative research pipeline](https://channel47.dev/notes/customer-research-ad-angles-claude).

## Start here

1. Unzip the folder and fill in what you know in brief.md.
2. Attach the completed brief and SKILL.md to your agent, or put them in a folder the agent can read.
3. Paste the prompt below, replacing the bracketed product and market details.

Use an agent with browsing to gather current public sources. If browsing is unavailable, supply the reviews, discussions, or research you want it to use. File creation is useful but optional: the agent can return the three documents as copyable sections. No installation is needed for the attachment-based path.

## Starting prompt

```text
Research why people buy [PRODUCT URL] in [MARKET]. Keep source links and separate evidence from assumptions.

Identify the main buyer types, then propose three ad ideas grounded in the research, with a hook and visual for each. Recommend one to develop first and flag anything that needs checking.
```

## What you get

- research.md: source material with evidence IDs, links, source types, themes, and gaps.
- personas.md: working buyer profiles tied to that evidence.
- angles.md: distinct creative ideas with draft hooks, visual directions, required proof, and questions for the owner.

Review the evidence and product facts before turning the ideas into ads. The kit supplies instructions and a blank brief; research and generation are performed by your agent.
