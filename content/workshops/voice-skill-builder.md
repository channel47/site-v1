This Vibe Marketers session used Dr. Squatch as a public example for building a
brand-voice skill in Claude Desktop.

## Topics covered

- Collecting representative writing samples.
- Identifying voice attributes and gathering missing context.
- Generating core instructions, editing guidance, and examples.
- Calibrating the skill with sample outputs before use.
- Packaging and installing a skill in Claude Desktop and Claude Code.
- Troubleshooting skill triggers and organizing skills by brand.
