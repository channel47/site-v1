Make Static Ads turns a campaign brief and real brand references into complete
static ad concepts, approved pilots, adaptive platform formats, and a packaged
delivery folder.

## Workflow

- Starts at ideation, production, or adaptation without repeating work that is
  already complete.
- Develops strategically different concepts instead of headline swaps.
- Generates one or two near-production pilots, discloses any deterministic
  corrections, then pauses for human approval.
- Recomposes approved concepts for standard paid-social placements instead of
  cropping one master into every ratio.
- Verifies filenames and dimensions, builds review sheets, and writes a
  campaign manifest before delivery.

## The production rule

The image model generates the complete creative, including the scene,
composition, and any typography required by the approved concept. It doesn't
default to generating a background that becomes an ad through overlays later.

Deterministic editing is reserved for precision corrections such as dimension
normalization, a localized typo, authentic logo replacement, product-faithful
packshot repair, or artifact removal.

![Two DrinkELT concepts generated with the product, scene, and typography integrated natively.](/posts/codex-static-ads-native-pass.jpg)

## Boundaries

- Supplied claims, offers, prices, reviews, and product details stay fixed.
- Product packaging is checked against trustworthy references when it appears.
- Material failures are regenerated before the work reaches the approval gate.
- The skill produces static raster creative, not video.
