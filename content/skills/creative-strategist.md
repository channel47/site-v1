Creative Strategist organizes customer research and creative development in a
single product dossier.

## Workflow

- Runs **research → personas → angles → advertorial** as separate stages or as
  one sequence.
- Writes each stage to `creative/[product-slug]-dossier.md`.
- Assigns stable IDs to sourced quotes and requires downstream claims to cite
  those IDs.
- Labels unsupported claims as speculative and records advertorial claims as
  proven, needs-source, or remove.
